Privacy Policy

TenantGuard
Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how TenantGuard ("the App"), made by Victain LTD, handles your information.

Information We Collect

TenantGuard helps tenants keep records of their tenancy. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the App's own storage. Victain LTD has no server for this App and never receives your records. This includes:

  • Properties, contacts (such as your landlord, letting agent or utility companies), and tenancy documents
  • Inspections, room-by-room inventories, issues and repairs, with any photos you take or choose
  • Utilities, meter readings and rent or bill payments

Data only leaves your phone when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a PDF report and sending it with another app. What happens to it then is up to you and that app.

Permissions

  • CAMERA – to photograph rooms, damage, meter readings and documents. Photos stay on your phone.
  • Photos you pick from your gallery use Android's system photo picker, so the App never gets access to your whole photo library.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – only on older Android versions (12 and below), to read and save files.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE – for reminders you set, such as bill or meter-reading dates, which are kept after a restart.
  • INTERNET – for ads and Google Play (purchases and updates). Your tenancy records are never sent over the internet by the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in the App. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID) and approximate location from your IP address to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Victain LTD never receives or stores your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase was made, so the App can unlock it.

Children's Privacy

TenantGuard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Rights

Data the App keeps on your phone is under your control: you can delete it in the App or by uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.
