Overview

Anywhen helps you arrange meetings across timezones. The developer (Victain LTD) does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline — the timezones, labels, contacts, and groups you set up stay entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following data is saved locally and never leaves your device:

  • Zone cards you've added (timezone, custom label)
  • Saved contacts and groups
  • Suggested/candidate meeting times you've entered
  • Theme preference (light/dark/system) and clock format (12/24-hour)
  • The pinned timezone shown in the optional home-screen widget

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays a small banner ad powered by Google AdMob. AdMob is an independent Google service that may collect your device's advertising identifier (Ad ID) to serve ads. This data is handled entirely by Google and is not accessible to Victain LTD.

You can opt out of personalised ads via your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+)
or Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation (older Android).

For more information see Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Anywhen offers an optional, one-time "buy me a coffee" tip. It unlocks nothing — it's just support, and can be repeated any number of times. It is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (or Apple's App Store on iOS); Victain LTD never receives or stores your payment information.

Sharing

Anywhen lets you share a link or summary of your board using your device's built-in share sheet. Sharing is entirely optional and initiated by you — we do not have access to what you share or who you share it with. A link generated by Anywhen uses the same format as the When's Good Website, so a link from either can be opened by the other.

Permissions used

  • INTERNET — to load the banner ad, process purchases, and check for app updates

No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device, including saved zones, contacts, groups, and preferences.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.
