Privacy Policy

BlockCheck: Delivery Block Pay
Last updated: 26 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how BlockCheck ("the App") handles your information. The App is published by Victain LTD ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the purposes of UK data protection law.

BlockCheck is an independent tool for delivery drivers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Amazon.

The Short Version

  • Everything you enter (your blocks, pay, offers, notes and settings) is stored only on your phone. We don't run a server and we never receive it.
  • Screenshots you scan are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Your home postcode is looked up once with a free postcode service to work out drive times.
  • The free version shows adverts from Google AdMob, which asks for your consent first.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • Home postcode and its map coordinates, used to estimate drive times to stations.
  • Car and cost settings: fuel type, MPG, fuel and electricity prices, target hourly rate and insurance rate.
  • Blocks you log: dates, stations, hours, pay, tips, mileage, fuel, insurance, paid status and notes.
  • Offers you save and station notes you write.
  • Stations added or corrected from your scanned screenshots.
  • Your weekly scan count and whether Premium is active.

We cannot see or access any of this. If your phone's Android backup to your Google account is switched on, Android may include the App's data in that backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

Screenshots

When you choose screenshots to scan (from the photo picker or by sharing them to the App), the text is read on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition. The images and their text are not uploaded or stored by us. Only the offer details the App recognises (station, date, time, length and pay) are kept, and only if you choose to save them.

Google ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device model, app details and performance metrics, to maintain and improve the service. It does not send your images. See Google's ML Kit terms.

Postcode Lookups

To find locations, the App sends postcodes to postcodes.io, a free UK postcode service. This covers:

  • your home postcode, when you enter or change it; and
  • station postcodes read from offer screenshots, to add or correct a station.

Only the postcode is sent. Like any web request, it includes your device's IP address. We do not receive or keep these lookups.

Advertising (Free Version)

The free version shows banner adverts from Google AdMob. Before any advert loads, the App asks for your consent using Google's consent tool, as required under UK law. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices.

AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with adverts. It uses this to show and measure adverts, and to prevent fraud. Depending on your consent, the adverts may be personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Premium users don't see adverts, and the advertising SDK doesn't load adverts for them.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime upgrade are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The App only checks with Google Play whether you own Premium. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

App Updates

The App uses Google Play's in-app update feature to tell you when a new version is available. This check is handled by Google Play on your device.

Backups and Exports You Create

If you use Save backup or Export CSV, the App creates a file and opens Android's share menu. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as your Google Drive or email.

Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: postcode lookups, adverts, purchases and update checks.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Premium purchases through Google Play.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS: added by Google AdMob to use the advertising ID and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad services for adverts (free version).
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: added by Google libraries for short background tasks, such as sending advert and diagnostic reports.

The App does not ask for access to all your photos, your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone. Screenshots are chosen by you through Android's photo picker or share menu.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete everything, clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App. There is no account and nothing held by us to delete.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because we don't hold your data, most requests can be handled directly on your device as described above. For anything else, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children's Privacy

BlockCheck is intended for adults (18 and over) who work as delivery drivers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Tax Figures

Tax and mileage figures in the App are estimates to help you keep records. They are not tax advice.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.

Contact
