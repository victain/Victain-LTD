Privacy Policy

Victain Tip
Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how Victain Tip ("the App"), made by Victain LTD, handles your information.

Information We Collect

Victain Tip is a tip calculator and bill splitter. The bills you split, the names you type for people in a group, and your saved groups are stored only on your phone. The App has no account and no server, and Victain LTD never receives this information.

A bill only leaves your phone when you choose to share it, for example to WhatsApp, using Android's share menu.

Permissions

  • CAMERA – to photograph a receipt so you can attach it to a bill. The photo stays on your phone unless you share it.
  • Receipts picked from your gallery use Android's system photo picker, so the App never gets access to your whole photo library.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – only on older Android versions, to read and save receipt images.
  • INTERNET – for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in the App. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID) and approximate location from your IP address to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Victain LTD never receives or stores your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase was made, so the App can unlock it.

Children's Privacy

Victain Tip is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Rights

Data the App keeps on your phone is under your control: you can delete it in the App or by uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.
