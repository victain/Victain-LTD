Privacy Policy

Business Hub
Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how Business Hub ("the App"), made by Victain LTD, handles your information.

Who the App Is For

Business Hub is an internal tool used by Victain LTD to run its own business. It is not offered to the public.

Information We Collect

The App stores Victain LTD's own business records on a server that Victain LTD runs itself (a self-hosted Supabase database in the UK). These records include:

  • Company profile, goals and projects
  • Clients and invoices, which may include client names and contact details
  • Transactions, subscriptions, dividends, assets and mileage logs
  • Compliance deadlines and notes
  • Documents and receipts uploaded through the App

This information is used only to run Victain LTD's accounts and meet its legal obligations (such as HMRC). It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Clients' Information

If you are a client of Victain LTD, your name, contact details and invoice history may be kept in Business Hub as part of our business records, for as long as UK law requires us to keep accounting records (normally 6 years). To ask what we hold about you or to correct it, contact us below.

Permissions

  • INTERNET – to read and save records on Victain LTD's server.
  • Files and photos are chosen with Android's system pickers; the App never gets access to the whole phone storage or photo library.

Third-Party Services

The App uses no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Business Hub is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Rights

Data the App keeps on your phone is under your control: you can delete it in the App or by uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: victain@mail.com or victain.org.
